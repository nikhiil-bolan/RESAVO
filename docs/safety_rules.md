# RESAVO Safety & Trust Rules

## Food Safety Policy
1. **No Image-Only Safety Certification**: An image cannot guarantee that cooked or perishable food is safe to consume. All food items must include preparation timestamp, storage conditions (refrigerated/ambient), and seller safety declaration.
2. **Perishable Expiry Windows**:
   - Fresh Cooked Food: Maximum 4 to 6 hours availability window depending on ambient temperature.
   - Dairy / Milk: Maximum 6 to 12 hours unless sealed or refrigerated.
   - Vegetables / Fruits: 24 to 72 hours based on visual condition.
3. **Organization Verification**: Bulk food redistribution to schools, NGOs, or institutional kitchens is allowed only after administrator verification of organization credentials.

## Privacy & Security
1. **Location Privacy**: Public search and match suggestions show approximate distance and fuzzing radius (300m - 500m). Exact household pickup address is revealed ONLY after both parties accept the match.
2. **Role-Based Access Control**: Users can act as Sellers or Buyers using role switching. Administrative endpoints require elevated privileges verified via JWT tokens.
